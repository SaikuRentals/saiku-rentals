Finance Overview

Total income includes your historical figure; profit and bank movements are calculated since system start.

Opening Bank Balance—Set your start date and opening bank balance in Settings.
Total Income Received—Historical income plus new rental payments
Total Business Expenses—Historical business expenses plus new expenses
Total Loan Payments—Historical repayments plus new repayments
Overall Net Profit—Total income minus business expenses, excluding loan principal

Historical figures are saved separately as a starting summary. New rental payments and expenses recorded on or after the system start date are added automatically. The opening bank balance is separate, so historical income is not counted again as money currently in the bank.

Cash Flow & Bank Deposits

Track cash collected by staff, expenses paid from cash, and deposits into the company bank account.

Cash Rental Payments—Cash received since system start
Cash Business Expenses—Expenses paid from collected cash
Cash Loan Payments—Loan repayments paid using cash
Deposited to Bank—Transfers from cash into bank
Cash Remaining from Tracked Activity—Cash receipts − cash outflows − deposits

A bank deposit is a transfer, not new income. The cash remaining figure only includes tracked movements since the system start date; opening cash held before that date has not been provided.
